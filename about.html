Our Story

A Passion for Beauty, Rooted in Coimbatore

Sri Kavi Beauty Parlour began as a small neighbourhood studio with a simple goal: to give every client honest, careful beauty care without the rush of a big salon chain. Over the years, word of mouth from happy clients turned that small studio into a full-service parlour trusted across Coimbatore.

Today, our team of trained stylists and beauticians continues that same philosophy — listening first, then delivering results that suit you, your skin and your occasion.
